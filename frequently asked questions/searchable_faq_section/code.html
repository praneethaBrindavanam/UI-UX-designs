Popular Questions

How do I reset my password?

expand_more

To reset your password, go to the login screen and click on 'Forgot Password'. You will receive an email with instructions on how to set a new one. Ensure you check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.

Can I change my subscription plan?

expand_more

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the 'Billing' section in your account settings. Changes are typically applied immediately or at the start of the next billing cycle depending on the plan.

What payment methods do you accept?

expand_more

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. For corporate accounts, we also support bank transfers upon request.
