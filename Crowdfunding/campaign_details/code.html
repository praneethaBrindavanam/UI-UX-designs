The Stewardship Mission

For nearly a millennium, the Watchtower has stood as a silent sentinel over the Northern Cliffs. Today, centuries of erosion and elemental battering have brought it to a critical tipping point. Without expert intervention, we risk losing this irreplaceable piece of our shared heritage.

Our restoration plan, developed by a world-class team of conservators and architectural historians, uses traditional masonry techniques paired with modern structural reinforcement. Every stone will be meticulously examined, every joint repointed with period-accurate lime mortar, and the foundations stabilized for the next 200 years.

Restoration Highlights

  • check_circle
    Structural Sealing: Preventing further water ingress into the core of the limestone walls.
  • check_circle
    Local Material Sourcing: Reopening the original quarry to match the specific geology of the 12th-century stone.
  • check_circle
    Educational Legacy: Establishing an on-site apprenticeship program for traditional stone-masonry skills.
