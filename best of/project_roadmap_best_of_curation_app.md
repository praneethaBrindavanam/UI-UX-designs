# "Best Of" Curation App - Project Overview

## Core Concept
A premium discovery and curation platform for "Best Of" lists across various categories (lifestyle, products, travel, etc.). The focus is on authority, beautiful presentation, and clear editorial "whys."

## Key User Flows
1. **Discovery:** Exploring trending and editorially curated "Best Of" lists.
2. **Deep Dive:** Reading the rationale behind a specific ranking or winner.
3. **Category Browsing:** Finding the best in specific niches.
4. **Social Sharing:** Engaging with community-driven rankings.

## Planned Screens
1. **Featured Home:** Editorial landing page with "The Daily Best" and top categories.
2. **Category Rankings:** A ranked list of items (1-10) with brief descriptions.
3. **Winner Profile:** Detailed view of a top-ranked item with rich media and expert verdict.
4. **Community Collections:** A feed of user-created "Best Of" lists.

## Visual Style
- **Editorial Aesthetic:** Large, impactful imagery and sophisticated typography.
- **Clear Hierarchy:** Bold numbers and badges for rankings.
- **Premium Feel:** Minimalist UI elements to let the content lead.
