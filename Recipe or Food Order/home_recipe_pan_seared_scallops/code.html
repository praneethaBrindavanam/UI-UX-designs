Main Course

Pan-Seared Scallops with Lemon Butter.

A masterclass in texture and simplicity. Achieving the perfect golden crust while maintaining a tender, buttery interior is the hallmark of professional kitchen execution.

Difficulty Intermediate
Serves 2 People

The Mise en Place

priority_high Allergy Alert

Contains Shellfish and Dairy. Ensure equipment is sanitized for cross-contamination safety.

Orchestration Steps

01
check_circle Prep Phase

The Moisture Removal

Remove the small, tough side muscle from the scallops if still attached. Pat the scallops bone-dry with paper towels. Any surface moisture will cause them to steam instead of sear. Season generously with salt just before cooking.

02
timer Active Sear

High-Heat Contact

Add oil to a large stainless steel pan over high heat. Once the oil begins to smoke slightly, add scallops one by one, leaving space between them. Do not move them for 2 minutes to develop a deep brown crust.

2:00
Heat Map Timer First Side Sear
03
restaurant Emulsification

The Butter Baste

Flip the scallops. Add the butter, lemon juice, and zest to the pan. Use a spoon to baste the scallops with the bubbling lemon butter for 1 minute. Remove immediately to a warm plate. Garnish with fresh herbs.
