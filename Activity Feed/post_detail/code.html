The Sun-Drenched Path of Gods

Marco Rossi

Travel Enthusiast • 2 days ago

"There is a moment, just as the sun dips behind the cliffs of Positano, where the entire world seems to turn into a golden watercolor painting."

The Sentiero degli Dei (Path of the Gods) isn't just a hike; it's a spiritual journey suspended between the sky and the sea. Starting from the quiet village of Agerola, the trail snakes through limestone ridges and ancient vineyards that have stood for centuries.

lightbulb

Pro Traveler Tip

Start your trek no later than 8:00 AM. Not only will you beat the Mediterranean heat, but you'll have the trail to yourself before the ferry crowds arrive from Sorrento. Don't forget to stop at the lemonade stand halfway!

As you descend toward Nocelle, the view opens up to reveal the entire coastline stretching toward Capri. Every step feels like a discovery, a quiet conversation with history and nature that leaves you breathless—and not just from the incline.
